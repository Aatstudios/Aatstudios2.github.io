Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about working with us.

How long does it take to get a response after submitting the contact form?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, we recommend calling us directly or reaching out via WhatsApp for a quicker response.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes, we offer complimentary initial consultations to discuss your project scope, requirements, and how we can help bring your vision to life. This initial meeting helps us understand your needs and provide an accurate proposal.

What information should I provide when contacting you about a project?

To help us better understand your project, please provide: project location, approximate size/budget, preferred timeline, and any specific requirements or inspiration images. The more details you can share, the better we can assist you.

Do you work on projects outside of Lagos?

Yes, we work on projects throughout Nigeria and internationally. We have experience managing remote projects through regular site visits, digital communication, and collaboration with local professionals when needed.
