Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and process.

How long does a typical architectural project take?

The timeline varies based on project scope and complexity. A small residential project might take 3-6 months from concept to construction documents, while larger commercial projects can take 6-18 months or more. We provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation.

What is your design philosophy?

We believe in creating spaces that are not only beautiful but also functional, sustainable, and responsive to their context. Our approach combines innovative design with practical solutions that enhance the quality of life for our clients and their communities.

Do you work on projects outside of Lagos?

Yes, we work on projects throughout Nigeria and internationally. We have experience managing remote projects through regular site visits, digital communication, and collaboration with local professionals when needed.

What is included in your service packages?

Our service packages typically include initial consultation, concept development, design development, construction documents, and construction administration. Additional services like 3D visualization, interior design, and project management can be added based on your needs.
